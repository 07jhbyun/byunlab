RESEARCH

Our research projects focus on the design of porous/conjugated polymers for energy & environmental applications.

Hover or tap a node to see details.

01

Energy

Conjugated Polymers for Visible-Light-Driven Photocatalysis

We are interested in polymeric photocatalysts that feature versatile photophysical and electronic properties through a fine structural design. We are trying to control their band structures and wetting properties for target-specific reactions, such as water splitting, oxygen reduction, nitrogen fixation, antibacterial treatment, etc.

02

Environment

Porous Polymers for Environmental Applications

We design the porous polymers by 'porosity' and 'chemistry' rules for selective adsorption of targeted compounds, e.g., CO2 molecules in the air, micropollutants in water, and organic vapors.
